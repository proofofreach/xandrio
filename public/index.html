Operator acknowledgement

This is your self-hosted instance

You control its books, sources, credentials, generated playback cache, and network connections. Xandrio is not a content provider or hosted TTS service.

Before using a source, narration engine, or voice reference, confirm that you have the authority required by applicable law and provider agreements. Source labels do not determine rights status.

Sources with unverified rights status remain disabled after this acknowledgement. Enable them separately in Settings only after reviewing the source and your intended use.
